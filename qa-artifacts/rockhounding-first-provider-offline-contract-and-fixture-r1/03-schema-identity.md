# Schema and identity

Record key scope: `STATE_SGMC_LABEL_UNIT_LINK_R1_COMPONENTS_NOT_A_GLOBAL_KEY`.

`OBJECTID` is `serviceObjectId` only.

`AGE_MIN` and `AGE_MAX` stay geologic attributes. The offline truth clock has no `retrievedAt` and no invented `sourceUpdatedAt`.

Coverage is conterminous United States. Alaska and Hawaii are excluded. Zero polygons do not establish geological absence. A 2000-record page with completion `UNKNOWN` is partial and unresolved.

Accepted display spatial references: `EPSG:3857` and `ESRI:102100`.
