# Governance and license

Reviewed 2026-09-23.

USGS-authored material is in the U.S. public domain. Embedded third-party copyright is a recorded caveat. Attribution is required. Endorsement is not implied.

`AUTOMATED_QUERY` is allowed with the attribution constraint.

`BULK_DOWNLOAD`, `PUBLIC_API`, `TRAINING_USE`, redistribution, local cache, offline package, model input, AI processing, and commercial use stay ungranted.

A READ receipt does not satisfy the adapter's AUTOMATED_QUERY requirement.
