# USGS public-domain policy

USGS Copyrights and Credits, retrieved 2026-09-23:

USGS-authored or produced data and information are considered U.S. public domain. Most USGS website content is public domain. Not every illustration or photograph on a USGS site is. Some non-USGS graphics are used with permission and are generally marked. Those marked materials need the copyright holder's permission.

USGS asks that proper credit be given when its information is used. The trademarked USGS identifier is governed separately and is not a substitute for a credit line.
